OUR JOURNEY

Turning Your House Into a Home

JB Furniture & Interior is a Pune-based furniture and interior business focused on creating customized solutions for modern homes.

From modular kitchens and wardrobes to TV units, custom furniture and complete interiors, we create solutions tailored to each customer's space, style and requirements.

Our approach is simple — understand the requirement, design around the available space, and deliver a solution that is practical, beautiful and made for everyday living.
